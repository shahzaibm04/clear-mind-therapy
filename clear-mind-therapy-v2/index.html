Common questions

Before you book.

Are sessions online or in person?

All sessions are online, through WhatsApp or Google Meet, so you can join from wherever you feel safe and comfortable.

Is what I share kept private?

Yes. What you share in session stays confidential with Mehwish. This website does not store the details you enter in the booking form.

What happens after I send the form?

Mehwish will contact you by phone or WhatsApp within 24 hours to agree on a time for your first session.

What are the fees?

Fees are shared when Mehwish contacts you.

I'm not sure which service I need.

That's completely fine. Choose “Not sure yet” in the form and share a few words about what you're facing — Mehwish will help you work out the right starting point.

What do I need for an online session?

A phone or computer with an internet connection, and a quiet, private spot where you feel comfortable.
